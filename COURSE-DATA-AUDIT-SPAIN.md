## Names

## Sotogrande region copy (regions.json), statements that look wrong or unsafe
1. "Nine 18-hole courses within six kilometres" (lede, stat "Courses within 6 km"): the nine pins (Valderrama, Real Sotogrande, San Roque Old, San Roque New, La Reserva, La Cañada, The Alto, Links, Heathland) are all within about 6 km of each other by map (widest pair Real Sotogrande to The Alto about 6.0 km). Defensible but "within 6 km" is of no stated centre; suggest "within about 10 minutes' drive". Note San Roque Old and New are now separate clubs, and the intro lists "San Roque Club" as one course.
2. "Gibraltar airport is ten minutes from the first tee at La Hacienda" / "10 min From Gibraltar airport": La Cañada's own site says 15 minutes from Gibraltar airport; La Hacienda is probably 15 too. Suggest "about 15 min". "Twenty from Sotogrande" is consistent with The Alto's site (20 min Gibraltar to Sotogrande).
3. "Málaga about an hour" / "60 min": plausible (La Cañada says about 90 km); keep "about an hour".
4. "our relationships with the clubs mean we can often arrange access that is hard to get": implies partnerships, against client rule 6. "We play these courses every week" unverifiable.
5. FAQ "Hacienda Links and Hacienda Heathland": fine as names. The Alto hit list text "Seven other top courses within ten minutes" was in the course record, now removed.
6. Valderrama "followed" the 1960s estate: Valderrama opened 1974 (as Las Aves), so "followed" is fine.

## Notes and flags

Source note: the Real Federacion Andaluza de Golf club portal (portal.golfandalucia.com) was used as an equivalent authoritative source for tee-by-tee metres, par, course rating, slope, designer and founding year. Club sites were used to cross-check where they publish figures.

- Villanueva Golf & Croquet: KEPT. It is a real 18-hole course in Puerto Real (pin is correct), with a visitor section and online booking. Par 71.
- Real Novo Sancti Petri: split into two records because the club and federation treat Course A (Mar y Pinos) and Course B (Centro) as separate courses. The extra slug is `real-novo-sancti-petri-centro`, sharing the map pin. A combined Mar + Centro routing also exists (black 6,450 m) and is not a separate record. The iberostar.com hotel page quotes 6,460 / 6,041 m; the federation figures were used.
- Costa Ballena: one 27-hole record (Olivos, Palmeras, Ficus) plus a 9-hole par 3. Par 72 figure is Olivos + Palmeras; the other combinations are par 70 (Palmeras + Ficus 5,714 m SR 125; Ficus + Olivos 5,802 m SR 128). Club publishes only yellow-tee lengths; the white-tee figure is from the federation. Opened shown as 1997 (club); federation says 1995.
- Sherry Golf Jerez: club text and federation both say 6,572 m, but the black row of the club's own scorecard sums to 6,600 m. 6,572 used. Designer shown as Stirling & Martin per the club; federation lists Global Golf Co.
- Fairplay: `opened` left blank (2001 federation vs 2002 club). Located in Benalup-Casas Viejas (Cádiz), not Tarifa or Vejer.
- Montenmedio: map name kept; resort now trades as Dehesa Montenmedio. Located on the N-340 at km 42.5, Vejer de la Frontera. Federation membership required.
- Arcos Gardens: current name on the club site is Arcos Golf (Arcos Gardens); official domain arcosgardensgolf.com. About 4 km from Arcos de la Frontera.
- La Estancia: designer spelling differs (club site "Allan Rijs", federation "Alan Rijks"); federation spelling used.
- Elba Costa Ballena: official site does not state a star rating; stars set to 0 and flagged for Max to confirm. Do not display as 4 without a source.
- Iberostar Royal Andalus: now branded Iberostar Waves Royal Andalus; 4 stars per the official site. No image available (iberostar.com blocked downloads).
- Barceló Montecastillo hotel: map had no lat/lon; none invented. Hotel records carry no lat/lon fields anyway.
- Facilities lists for Arcos Golf, Villanueva (croquet lawns come from the club name and site), Sherry and Sancti Petri Hills are generic items that the club pages support at a general level; items such as 'Pro shop' for Arcos and Villanueva were not individually confirmed and should be checked.
- Region stats: 11 courses = 10 assigned plus the Centro record. 58 min / 64 km Jerez to Sancti Petri Hills and 1 h 40 Sevilla are from the Sancti Petri Hills site. Green fee range EUR 78 / 86 / 94 and buggy EUR 30 / 35 are the club's published 2026 rates.
- Map pins unchanged for all courses and hotels.
